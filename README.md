# ER:LC Vehicle Lookup & Logging Assistant

An essential web-based roleplay utility designed for **Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC)** communities. Built specifically for **Law Enforcement Officers (LEO)**, **Department of Transportation (DOT)** personnel, and server members to quickly identify, catalog, and log vehicles during shift duties.

---

## 🚘 Key Features

* **Instant Vehicle Identification:** Easily look up vehicle names, exact models, and production/in-game years.
* **Visual Catalog:** Full screenshot references to help identify vehicles on sight during patrols or calls.
* **BOLO & Shift Logging:** Perfect for LEOs verifying details before issuing a BOLO or DOT staff logging impounds and tow requests.
* **Fast & Accessible:** Lightweight web interface built for quick references in the middle of active roleplay scenarios.
